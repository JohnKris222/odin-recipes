How to make Moussaka

There’s 4 components to Moussaka:

  1. cooking the eggplant;
  2. the meat sauce, a rich Bolognese type sauce made with lamb or beef but with traditional Greek flavours of oregano and cinnamon;
  3. thick béchamel sauce – thicker than used in Lasagna and things like Broccoli Gratin, it’s semi-set using eggs;
  4. layering it all up, lasagna style.

Incidents

Eggplant

  • 1 kg / 2 lb eggplant (aubergines) , 0.75cm / 0.3″ thick slices
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 – 3 tbsp olive oil

Filling

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced (brown, white, or yellow)
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 700 g (1.4 lb) ground beef or lamb (mince)
  • 1/2 cup dry red wine (optional)
  • 400 g (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 cup beef broth/stock
  • 1 beef bouillon cube, crumbled (or 1 tsp powder)
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1.5 tsp sugar
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon (or 1 cinnamon stick)
  • 3/4 tsp salt

Filling

  • 4 tbsp (60 g) butter
  • 5 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 1/2 cups milk (any fat %)
  • 1/4 tsp freshly grated nutmeg (optional)
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan (or Kefalotiri cheese)
  • 1 egg
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 1/4 tsp Vegeta (vegetable or chicken stock powder) or salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Bechamel Sauce

  • 4 tbsp (60 g) butter
  • 5 tbsp plain flour
  • 2½ cups milk (any fat %)
  • ¼ tsp freshly grated nutmeg (optional)
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan or Kefalotiri cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1¼ tsp Vegeta (vegetable or chicken stock powder), or salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Topping

  • 1/3 cup panko breadcrumbs

Instructions

Eggplant

  1. Place the eggplant slices slightly overlapping in a large colander. Sprinkle with salt, then repeat with the remaining eggplant.
  2. Leave to sweat for 30 minutes. Meanwhile, prepare the Meat Sauce and Béchamel Sauce.
  3. Preheat the oven to 240°C (450°F).
  4. Pat the eggplant dry thoroughly to remove excess salt. Arrange on parchment-lined baking trays and brush with olive oil.
  5. Bake for 15–20 minutes, or until lightly browned and softened. Remove from the oven and set aside to cool slightly.

Meat Sauce

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet or pot over high heat. Cook the onion and garlic for 2 minutes.
  2. Add the ground beef or lamb and cook until browned, breaking it up as it cooks.
  3. Pour in the red wine and cook for about 1½ minutes, until the alcohol smell disappears.
  4. Add the remaining sauce ingredients and stir well to combine.
  5. Bring to a simmer, reduce the heat to medium-low, and cook for 15 minutes until the sauce is thick.

Béchamel Sauce

  1. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the flour and cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly.
  2. Gradually whisk in the milk while stirring continuously.
  3. Cook for 3–5 minutes, stirring regularly, until the sauce thickly coats the back of a wooden spoon.
  4. Remove from the heat and whisk in the Parmesan, nutmeg, Vegeta (or salt), and black pepper.
  5. Let the sauce cool for 5 minutes, then whisk in the egg and egg yolk. Cover until ready to use.

Assemble

  1. Reduce the oven temperature to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Arrange half of the baked eggplant in the bottom of a baking dish.
  3. Spread the Meat Sauce evenly over the eggplant.
  4. Top with the remaining eggplant slices.
  5. Pour the Béchamel Sauce over the top and sprinkle with breadcrumbs.
  6. Bake for 30–40 minutes, or until golden brown.
  7. Let the moussaka rest for 10 minutes before serving.
