French Grated Carrot Salad (Carottes Rapées)

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450 g) carrots, peeled and grated (about 3½ cups)
  • 3 tbsp flat-leaf parsley, stems removed and chopped
  • 2 tbsp raisins (optional)
  • 2 tbsp pine nuts (optional)
  • 3 tbsp (45 ml) extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp (30 ml) lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp (20 ml) Dijon mustard
  • ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper, or to taste
  • ½ tsp salt, or to taste

Instructions

  1. Place the grated carrots in a large bowl and add the chopped parsley, pine nuts, and raisins.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, black pepper, and salt.
  3. Pour the vinaigrette over the carrots and toss until everything is evenly coated.
  4. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed.
  5. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and refrigerate for 30 minutes to allow the flavors to develop and the carrots to soften slightly.
  6. Toss again just before serving.
